Boilerplate

One line

Sava Labs is a UK software company building self-serve learning products that help people across Europe prepare for high-stakes exams.

Short

Sava Labs Ltd is a privately held software company registered in England and Wales. It builds and operates a portfolio of focused consumer products for learning and exam preparation, each dedicated to a single country and a single exam, with an emphasis on civic knowledge and citizenship tests across Europe.

Extended

Sava Labs Ltd is a privately held UK software company founded in 2024. The company designs, builds, and operates consumer web and mobile products for learning and exam preparation, with an emphasis on civic knowledge and citizenship tests across Europe. Each product serves one country and one exam, in the languages its learners actually speak, and is built to a simple standard: accurate content grounded in official sources, honest pricing, and software that respects the person using it. Sava Labs is independent and founder-owned, and operates its products under their own brand names.

Typography

Display and quotations are set in an old-style serif (Iowan Old Style, Palatino, or Georgia). Everything else is set in a neutral system sans-serif. Labels are small, uppercase, and letterspaced. Restraint over decoration: one serif, one sans, hairline rules, generous whitespace.

Usage

  • The company name is written "Sava Labs", two words; the legal name is "Sava Labs Ltd".
  • Use the marks as provided. Do not recolor outside the palette, distort, add effects, or place on busy backgrounds.
  • Keep clear space around the marks of at least the height of the letter S.
  • Sava Labs products are operated under their own brand names; this identity is for the company itself.
